- 2026-11-07 — PHASE 3 LAB PARAMETERS (fixed before any agent enters the lab). Delay conditioning, ISI 6 (CS on for 7 ticks, US at CS onset + 6; matches the ecology's delay-cue structure); ITI U{15..35}; contingency 1.0 for CS+.
