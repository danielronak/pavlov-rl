- 2026-11-07 — PHASE 3 LAB PARAMETERS (fixed before any agent enters the lab). Delay conditioning, ISI 6 (CS on for 7 ticks, US at CS onset + 6; matches the ecology's delay-cue structure); ITI U{15..35}; contingency 1.0 for CS+. Bell and metronome = 2 distinct random channels per subject; the other 2 channels stay silent. Protocol lengths are chosen so the full Pavlov protocol ≈ one training lifetime (~3000 ticks): naive 4 CS+ + 4 CS− (no US); acquisition 25 CS+→US + 25 CS− (shuffled); extinction 20 CS+; rest 300 blank ticks; recovery 5 CS+. Omission and positive-contingency protocols: naive + 40 CS+ (contingent) + 20 CS−, each with a yoked partner that replays the delivered food exactly. CR = mean p(saliva) over the 3 ticks before the (would-be) US minus mean p over the 10 ticks before CS onset. OSI = clip(1 − CR_omission / CR_yoked, 0, 1) over the last 15 CS+ trials, computed on subject-averaged CRs. 32 lab subjects per seed. Change from the plan draft (40 acquisition trials, 30 extinction trials, 600 rest ticks): shortened for the lifetime-length constraint, not for any result — I5, I6, I7, I8 — n
